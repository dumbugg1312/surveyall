Each family is a variable-weight WOFF2 built from the upstream sources
in github.com/google/fonts and self-hosted here, so the app makes no
external network requests and renders identically on every machine.

Each is split in two on unicode-range, the way Google Fonts serves them:
`<Family>-Variable.woff2` covers the `latin` subset, and
`<Family>-Variable-ext.woff2` covers Latin Extended, so an ASCII-only
deck never downloads the second file. Two families carry a second
variation axis that is pinned rather than shipped live, because nothing
in the app varies it: Source Serif 4 at opsz=20 (its text setting) and
Fraunces at SOFT=0, WONK=1 (the upstream defaults). Inter is pinned at
opsz=14 to match the build that shipped before the split.
