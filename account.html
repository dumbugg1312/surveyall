Create an instructor account

You'll need the sign-up code from whoever runs this site.

3–32 characters, starting with a letter or a number. After that, letters, numbers, dots, dashes and underscores are all fine. It doesn't have to be your real name.

At least 4 characters — a short PIN is fine. There is no reset email, so pick something you'll remember; only an admin can set you a new one.

We store your username and a hashed password. No email, no name, no tracking. What we store and why.
